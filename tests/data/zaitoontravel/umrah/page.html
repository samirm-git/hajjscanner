Swissotel Al Maqam Makkah

( Hotel in makkah )

Perfect staying option especially when you are embarking on a Holy journey and seeking everything luxurious and comfortable.Situated at the heart of the Muslim world, overlooking the holy city. It is part of the well-known Abraj Al Bait complex and offers a direct view of the holy Kaaba, as well as access to Masjid Al Haram. The hotel's rooms and suites have a warm and welcoming ambiance, with many of them offering a beautiful view of the holy Kaaba.
