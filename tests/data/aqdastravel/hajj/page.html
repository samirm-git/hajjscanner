Makkah Hotel

VOCO MAKKAH 

  • ⭐ 3 Star Hotel

  • ♿ Wheelchair Friendly

  • 🚶 ~20–25 minutes walking distance (Located in Ibrahim Al Khalil Street)

  • 🚌 24/7 Dedicated Shuttle Service to Masjid al-Haram

  • ❄️ Air-Conditioned Rooms

  • 📶 Free Wi-Fi Available

  • 🍽️ Breakfast – Optional / Can Be Included

voco Makkah is a premium property that offers a high-end experience, often exceeding standard 3-star expectations. While it is located a bit further down Ibrahim Al Khalil Street, it is famous for its exceptionally frequent and efficient 24-hour shuttle service, which drops pilgrims off very close to the Haram. The hotel features stylish, contemporary rooms with signature voco bedding, large bathrooms, and modern technology. It is one of the largest hotels in the area, offering multiple dining outlets, massive prayer halls, and a spacious lobby designed to handle large numbers of guests smoothly. The property is fully wheelchair accessible and is an ideal choice for those who prefer modern, upscale comfort and are happy to utilize a quick shuttle for Haram access.

Madinah Hotel

NUSK AL MADINAH HOTEL

  • ⭐ 3 Star Hotel

  • ♿ Wheelchair Friendly

  • 🚶 ~5–7 minutes walking distance from Al-Masjid an-Nabawi

  • 🌆 City View Rooms

  • ❄️ Air-Conditioned Rooms

  • 📶 Free Wi-Fi Available

  • 🍽️ Breakfast – Optional / Can Be Included

Nusk Al Madinah is a reliable 3-star choice located in the Northern Central Area of Madinah. It is well-regarded for providing a high standard of cleanliness and comfort at an affordable price point. The hotel is situated within a convenient walking distance to the Prophet’s Mosque, making it easy for guests to reach the Haram for all prayers. Rooms are simply but elegantly furnished, featuring air conditioning, flat-screen TVs, and en-suite bathrooms. The property is fully wheelchair accessible and offers helpful 24-hour front desk service. Its proximity to the main shopping streets and various local dining options makes Nusk Al Madinah a practical and welcoming base for pilgrims and visitors looking for a straightforward, high-value stay.
