Frequently Questions? Answered!

How do I register for Hajj through Nusuk from France?

We handle the entire Nusuk process for you. Once you register with Sara International Travel, our team guides you step-by-step through account creation, document uploads, package selection and final purchasing.

What’s included in your Hajj package for French travelers?

Our packages include:

  • Round-trip airfare from major French Airports like  CDG, ORY, MRS, and LYS
  • Visa & Nusuk registration assistance
  • 5-star hotel stays in Makkah and Madinah
  • Accommodations in Mina and Arafat
  • Guided rituals by French-speaking scholars
  • Meals, ground transport, and group support
Is it difficult to check available Hajj slots for France?

Yes, many pilgrims from France report limited visibility into quota updates. With Sara International Travel, you’ll receive real-time updates, clear instructions, and priority alerts when new spots open.

What documents and vaccinations do I need as a French pilgrim?

You’ll need a valid French passport (machine-readable, 6+ months validity), and proof of residence. Vaccinations include Meningitis ACYW135 on your WHO Yellow Card.

Can I choose my own Murshid or group leader for Hajj 2027?

Yes. When booking through Sara International Travel, you can choose to travel with one of our designated Murshids, based on the group package you select.

However, please note that Nusuk may occasionally make changes to the designated Murshids based on their internal protocols.

What is the cost of a Nusuk Hajj 2027 package from France?

With Sara International Travel costs vary depending on the package types. On average, prices range from €9,500–€13,800 EUR for full-service packages with 5-star accommodations, meals, and guided services.

Can I receive support in French during the Hajj process?

Yes. Our team includes French-speaking staff and scholars, and we provide pre-Hajj webinars, documentation, and WhatsApp support entirely in French. With Sara International, you’ll never feel lost in translation.

What is the refund and cancellation policy for Nusuk Hajj 2027 bookings?

The cancellation fees for Nusuk Hajj packages depend on when the cancellation is made. There’s a general 2.3% processing fee plus VAT deduction when funds are withdrawn from the Nusuk wallet. If the Hajj permit is issued and the cancellation happens between the 15th of Shawwal and the end of Dhul Qadah (9 days before the climax of Hajj), 10% of the contract value is deducted. If the cancellation occurs on or after the first of Dhul Hijjah (the month of Hajj), the entire contract value is forfeited.
