Abeer Al Azizia Hotel ( Hotel In makkah ) 3.2 (205 Reviews)

Abeer Al Azizia Hotel is an excellent choice for travelers visiting Makkah, offering a budget-friendly environment alongside many helpful amenities designed to enhance your stay. Just a 10-minute drive from the Holy Haram, Abeer Al Azizia Hotel offers air-conditioned accommodations with free Wi-Fi. Each room has a flat-screen satellite TV, seating area, electric kettle, and fridge. Furnished with a warm décor, the elevator-accessible units at Abeer Al Azizia Hotel include a bathroom with a shower. King Abdulaziz International Airport is 75 minutes away by car. Free private parking is possible on-site. You can walk to Bawarith Shopping Centre in a few minutes. During your stay here, enjoy the unlimited amenities:

Manazel Alaswaf Hotel ( Hotel In medina ) 3.5 (235 Reviews)

Located in Medina, Manazel Alaswaf Hotel offers simply furnished accommodations. Free WiFi access is available throughout. Al Masjid Al Nabawi is a 5-minute walk away.
Each room here will provide you with a flat-screen TV, air conditioning and a minibar. There is also an electric kettle.
Featuring a bath or shower, private bathroom also comes with a hairdryer and free toiletries. You can enjoy city view from the room.
At Manazel Alaswaf Hotel you will find a 24-hour front desk.
The hotel is 1.6 miles from Madina Mall and 2.4 miles from Al Noor Mall.

  • Family rooms
