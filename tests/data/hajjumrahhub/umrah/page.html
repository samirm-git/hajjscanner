Hotel In Madinah

Located in the central Madinah district of Al Madinah, Grand Zowar Hotel is approximately a 6-minute walk (about 1,969 feet) from Al-Masjid an-Nabawi and close to popular attractions such as the Old Bazaar (1.3 miles) and Mazaya Mall (1.6 miles). The hotel features a restaurant, a 24-hour front desk, room service, and complimentary WiFi, with private parking available at an additional cost. All rooms are air-conditioned and equipped with a flat-screen TV with satellite channels, a refrigerator, a kettle, a wardrobe, and a private bathroom with a shower and free toiletries, and many rooms offer views of the city. Al Baqia Cemetery is 2.1 miles away, Quba Mosque is 2.3 miles from the property, and Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport is located 11 miles from the hotel.
