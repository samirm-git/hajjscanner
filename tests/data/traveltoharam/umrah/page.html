Accomandations Features

Hotel In Makkah

Almassa Grand Hotel

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

  • Situated in Mecca City, Almassa Grand Hotel provides contemporary, sleek lodging within a short distance of Holy Masjid Al-Haram. Guests can appreciate room amenities, including plush carpeting, modern furnishings, a flat-panel television, and a mini-bar. Every ensuite is furnished with a stand-alone shower. Guests are offered room service, easy access to surrounding business districts, and even a 24 hour concierge service. King Abdulaziz international airport is around a 75 minute drive and can be reached in under 10 minutes. Al Azizia District is exactly 10 minutes.

Hotel In Madina

Mirage AlSalam

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

  • Mirage Al Salam is right in the middle of Madinah and is only a short walk away from Al Masjid An Nabawi. Each room is set with equipped with a carpet, contemporary design, and a mini bar with a flat screen TV. For guest’s comfort, the hotel offers a private bathroom that offers a set of free toiletries and a private shower. Other offered services include , on site restaurant, room service available for 24 hours and stores within the vicinity. The hotel is a 10 minute drive from the Quba Mosque, and 25 minutes from Prince Muhammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport.
