Raffles Makkah Palace ( Hotel in makkah )

  • ( 5 Star )

Located in the prestigious Abraj Al-Bait complex overlooking the Grand Mosque and Kaaba shrine, Raffles Makkah Palace boasts spacious suites with panoramic views and 24-hour personal butler service. The property is only 50 meters away from Makkah Clock Tower. Guests can enjoy free WiFi in all areas of the hotel. All large and air-conditioned suites boast luxurious décor with marbled floors and oriental carpeting. Each unit has a large living room and a private bathroom. The Raffles Makkah Palace serves a wide range of cuisine at 2 different restaurants. A tea lounge and ice cream-based café are also on the hotel grounds. The Levantine Al Majlis' fine dining restaurant provides a traditional Mediterranean-influenced experience. The terrace overlooks the Grand Mosque and the Ka'aba. Guests can relax at Makkah Palace’s serene spa or work out in the separate male or female gym. The hotel has numerous private places where guests can quietly read a book or socialise. Jeddah International Airport is just over an hour's drive away. The south entrance of the Grand Mosque complex is located just across the street from the hotel.

MADEN HOTEL ( Hotel in madina )

  • ( 5 Star )

Situated 100 metres from Al Haram, MADEN Hotel features classic guest rooms with chic carpeted floors, air conditioning and a flat-screen TV. Guests can enjoy views of Al Masjed Al Nabawi from the property's two restaurant. Featuring free WiFi access, the spacious rooms at MADEN Hotel come with a minibar and direct telephone. Each room has an en-suite bathroom with a hairdryer and selected shower products. The 24-hour front desk staff at MADEN Hotel can facilitate room service, and takes care of laundry and ironing requests. The hotel is a 10-minute drive from the public station. Prince Mohamed Bin Abdul Aziz Airport is a 25-minute drive away.
