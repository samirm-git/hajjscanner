Al Saher Hotel

About Property

Just a 10-minute drive from Al Tahliya shopping street, Al Saher for Hotel Apartment offers air-conditioned accommodation with flat-screen TV. Free Wi-Fi is available in all areas.

Fitted with dark wooden furniture and parquet floors, all accommodations at Al Saher feature a modern décor. Each one includes a telephone and an electric kettle. The apartment offers a living room and a kitchen complete with fridge and stove.

Nearby restaurants are located within a walking distance from Al Saher for Hotel Apartment. King Abdulaziz Airport is 10 minutes by car. Free private parking is possible on site.
