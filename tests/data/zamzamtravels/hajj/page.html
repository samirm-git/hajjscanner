We have tailored this 5* Star non-Shifting Hajj Package with the utmost care and attention to detail. We aim to provide you with a package tour, promising a great experience, In shāʾ Allāh.

Your 15-day, 5* Star Hajj Package in 2022 will depart with our late group. You will begin your Hajj Package in Makkah before continuing onto al-Madīnah, where you will complete your Hajj pilgrimage.

This Hajj package provides you with the opportunity to reside in one of Makkah's most luxurious hotels, the 5* Star Elaf Kinda, Makkah (or Swissotel/Royal Dar Al Eimaan/Al Ghufran Safwah Hotel) and al-Madīnah's 5* Star Anwar al Madinah Mövenpick Hotel (or Sofitel - formerly Shaza Madinah). Both of your hotels reside within a few footsteps of our holy sanctuaries. Your stay will be half board (breakfast and dinner).

We'll ensure that you spend enough time absorbing the atmosphere in both sanctuaries and experience ziyārat mentioned below.

If it's your first time visiting the Holy Lands, rest assured, our fully qualified scholars will be available to guide you on the steps needed to perform Hajj in 2022 (AH 1443). If you're one of our frequent visitors, they'll be available for questions or useful reminders.

Itinerary

This 5* Star, 15-day Hajj 2022 Package itinerary (if departure is on 2nd July 2022) is as follows:

01 2nd July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 3, 1443 AH

Meet & Assist at your Airport and fly to Jeddah

Our team will meet you at the airport in the United Kingdom. From there, we will fly together to Jeddah Airport in Saudi Arabia. We will be available to assist throughout your ATOL Protected journey.

02 3rd July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 4, 1443 AH

Arrive in Makkah

Arrive in Makkah and check in to your hotel. You will be free to spend your day in al-Masjid al-Ḥarām, where you will be free to spend your time resting and focusing on your Ibadah.

Please note: When we arrive as a group at Jeddah Airport, we will have to undergo immigration. It is not uncommon to spend more than six hours getting through immigration control during the Hajj period.

03 4th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 5, 1443 AH

A day to rest and pray in Makkah

Spend your time in Makkah, focusing on your prayers. You'll be free to enjoy the sights and sounds of Makkah.

04 5th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 6, 1443 AH

A day to rest and pray in Makkah

Spend your time in Makkah, focusing on your prayers. You'll be free to enjoy the sights and sounds of Makkah.

05 6th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 7, 1443 AH

Leave for Mina

We will leave for Mina, where we will spend the next few days. There you can continue focusing on your prayers and preparing for the days ahead as we prepare for the main Hajj rites to begin.

06 7th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 8, 1443 AH

A day of rest in Mina

Today will be your first full day in Mina. Enjoy the atmosphere in Mina and spend your time focusing on your prayers and participate in lectures and guidance.

07 8th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 9, 1443 AH

Yawm ‘Arafah, the second day of Hajj

After Fajr salat, at approximately 8:00 am, we will leave Mina and journey to Mount Arafah, the Mountain of Mercy. We will spend the day here until Maghrib salat, focusing on prayers and dua. We will then journey to Muzdalifah where we will remain until Fajr salat.

08 9th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 10, 1443 AH

Leave Muzdalifah for stoning at Jamarāt

After Fajr, we will leave Muzdalifah and return to Mina. After arriving at Mina, we will depart as a group for stoning at Jamarāt.

Once we have completed stoning at Jamarāt, we will journey to Makkah to complete Tawaf-Al-Ziyarah and return to Makkah.

Upon arriving at Makkah, we will await news for the completion of Qurbanī. After this, you will be free to release your iḥrām. After Maghrib, we will return to Mina where you can rest.

Please note: Today will be the most physically demanding day for your Hajj 2022 package pilgrimage. We strongly suggest preparing yourself for walking in the region of 15 miles (25km). You will need suitable footwear for today. Ask our team for any recommendations.

09 10th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 11, 1443 AH

A second day of stoning at Jamarāt

We will have the opportunity to complete stoning at Jamarāt. When we've finished stoning, we will travel back to Mina. At Mina, spend the remainder of your day resting, praying, performing your duahs, and listening to lectures.

10 11th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 12, 1443 AH

A third day of stoning at Jamarāt

We will have the opportunity to complete a third day of stoning at Jamarāt. When we've finished stoning, we will travel back to Makkah. At Makkah, spend the remainder of your day resting, praying, performing your duahs, and listening to lectures.

Quick note: If you wish, you can stay in Mina for another night and spend the following day stoning at Jamarāt.

11 12th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 13, 1443 AH

A day to rest and pray in Makkah

Spend your time in Makkah, focusing on your prayers. You'll be free to enjoy the sights and sounds of Makkah.

12 13th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 14, 1443 AH

Journey to al-Madīnah

Spend your day resting and focusing on your prayers until our transport to al-Madīnah arrives.

Upon the arrival of our transport, we will leave Makkah and journey to al-Madīnah via coach that the Hajj Ministry provisions. Once we arrive in al-Madīnah, we will check in to your 5* Star hotel.

Once checked in, you will be free to focus on your 'ibādah and explore Al-Masjid an-Nabawī and the surrounding sights and sounds of al-Madīnah. Our team will be available for assistance, should you need it.

Quick note: The journey to al-Madīnah, inclusive of rest stops and traffic, can take in the region of ten hours. As part of your non-Shifting package, we will provide you with a private VIP coach for the journey.

13 14th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 15, 1443 AH

Rest in al-Madīnah

You will be free for the entire day to focus on your 'ibādah and explore Al-Masjid an-Nabawī and the surrounding sights and sounds of al-Madīnah. Our team will be available for assistance, should you need it.

14 15th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 16, 1443 AH

Journey to Jeddah

Spend your morning in al-Madīnah, focusing on your prayers and resting.

Once our transport arrives at around 2pm, it will mark the end of your Hajj Package in 2022. You will check out from your hotel in al-Madīnah, and we will travel to Jeddah Airport.

15 16th July 2022 Dhuʻl-Hijjah 17, 1443 AH

Return to the United Kingdom

Upon arrival at Jeddah Airport, you will make your way back to the United Kingdom.
