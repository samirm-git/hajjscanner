Accomandations Features

Hotel in Makkah

Al Safwah Royale Orchid

Al Safwah Royale Orchid is a 5-star hotel in Makkah, just steps from the Grand Mosque. It offers modern, spacious rooms with amenities like minibars, prayer rugs, and luxury bathrooms. The hotel features multiple restaurants, a café, 24-hour room service, and family-friendly facilities. With a multilingual front desk and accessibility options, it’s ideal for pilgrims seeking comfort and convenience. Prices can be higher during peak seasons, and parking is limited.

Hotel in Madina

Hotel Pullman Zamzam Madinah

Pullman Zamzam Madinah is a premium hotel ideally located near the Prophet’s Mosque. It offers luxury accommodations with a blend of Arabic and French design, making it a popular choice for pilgrims and visitors who want to stay close to the Haram.
The hotel has over 800 rooms and suites. They are modern, spacious, and well-furnished. Some rooms offer views of the Haram or the Green Dome, while others face the city or nearby buildings.
