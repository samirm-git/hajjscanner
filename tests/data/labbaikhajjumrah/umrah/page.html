Package Details

The 10 Nights 5 Star Luton Package is a premium extended year-round Luton Umrah package routed from London and covering 11 days and 10 nights in Saudi Arabia, structured as a Madinah-first itinerary with a 5-night opening stay in Madinah al-Munawwarah followed by a 5-night stay in Makkah al-Mukarramah, with arrival routing through Prince Mohammad Bin Abdulaziz Airport (Madinah) and departure routing through King Abdulaziz International Airport (Jeddah) for a logistically optimal split-airport flow that minimises road travel.


The package places pilgrims at Sofitel Shahd Al Madinah and Swissotel Makkah Hotel, and is built for Luton-based and Bedfordshire worshippers seeking the traditional Madinah-first spiritual sequence on a premium 5-star extended Umrah from Luton at exceptionally Haram-adjacent globally branded Accor hotels in both holy cities — Swissotel at just 150 metres from Masjid al-Haram and Sofitel at 200 metres from Masjid an-Nabawi — with London airport access via the Luton-to-London St Pancras train (~30 minutes direct) for Saudia Airlines' established Madinah-first inventory, couples and small groups travelling together in Triple Room luxury, and Luton Muslim community pilgrims wanting a premium Umrah Packages Luton option with bundled flights, private transport, dedicated guide, and included guided ziyarah handled in exchange for arranging their own visa.


Pricing for this 10 Nights 5 Star Luton Package starts from £1,303 per person, positioned competitively for a premium-tier extended Luton-targeted Umrah Package.


Inclusions in the 10 Nights 5 Star Luton Package: outbound flight from London with Saudia Airlines into Prince Mohammad Bin Abdulaziz Airport (Madinah) and return flight from King Abdulaziz International Airport (Jeddah), private air-conditioned transport across all airport and intercity transfers covering the Madinah-airport arrival transfer, the Madinah-to-Makkah road journey, and the Makkah-to-Jeddah-airport departure transfer, a dedicated Umrah guide supporting pilgrims through every rite from Ihram and Talbiyah at Miqat to the closing Halq or Taqsir, and guided ziyarah tours covering historical religious sites in both Makkah and Madinah at no additional cost. Umrah Visa processing is not included with this package and must be arranged separately by the pilgrim.
